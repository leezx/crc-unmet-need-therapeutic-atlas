
# 2026 CRLM PDO biobank

Publication: `PMID:42208542`, “Patient-derived organoids from metastatic colorectal cancer mirror tumor heterogeneity and predict patient survival and drug sensitivity”. The study reports 213 organoids derived from liver metastases of 102 patients with metastatic CRC.

## Official processed-data source

Mendeley Data v3: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/hr94h42xdc/3  
DOI: `10.17632/hr94h42xdc.3`  
License: CC BY 4.0.  
Download method: use the Mendeley Data landing page’s `Download All` control; exact file-level metadata is indexed in `file_inventory.tsv`. No file was downloaded for this registry update.

The repository exposes five processed/derived data files: Data S1 somatic mutation profiles, Data S2 RNA-seq gene-expression counts, Data S3 multiplex-IHC protein expression, Data S4 raw drug-screen well measurements, and Data S5 drug-sensitivity scores. The files share `sample_id` and are relevant to treatment-response and molecular-context analysis.

## Admission boundary

This candidate remains `P0_DOWNLOAD` / `CANDIDATE`. The registry does not claim that the Mendeley files contain raw sequencing or raw proteomics, does not infer systemic treatment history from organoid drug screens, and does not approve the dataset until sample-level clinical context, exact downloaded-file checksums and third-party terms are reviewed.
