> **`NECTIN4` shows low RNA detection fractions in both Module B cohorts (`GSE178318` and `GSE225857`) — does protein-level evidence also show a limited, target-specific signal, or contradict it?**

See `data_lock/tgt_nectin4.md`: shows a limited signal too, on its own terms. `PXD055821` MS detects `NECTIN4` in 13/60 specimens (21.7%). `HPA_CRC_cancer_tissue` scores mostly `Low`/`Not detected` (8 of 10 patients: 0 High, 1 Medium, 7 Low, 2 Not detected). These are `NECTIN4`'s own numbers from two independent protein-layer sources, reported on their own terms — not compared against the other four targets, and not combined into one score across measurement types. ADC precedent for `NECTIN4` is recorded in Module A and not restated here.
