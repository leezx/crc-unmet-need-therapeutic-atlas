# Module E question — `tgt_ceacam5`

First real target run through Module E (2026-08-23), picked from the five `ADC_TARGET_SEED_UNIVERSE.tsv` targets with documented CRC/mCRC trial precedent (`DATA/registry/ADC_TARGET_SEED_UNIVERSE.tsv`): `CEACAM5` has the most CRC-specific precedent of the five (Labetuzumab govitecan is a CEACAM5-directed ADC with CRC-specific indication text, vs. the pan-tumor precedent of `ERBB2`/`F3`/`NECTIN4`/`TACSTD2`).

> **明显不值得继续的正常组织 liability——CEACAM5 是否在 colon/rectum 以外的正常组织里有明显的 accessible target-positive 群体？**

Not asking for a safety score. Asking whether bulk-RNA screening surfaces a normal-tissue compartment worth a closer (protein/IHC) look before any ADC-repurposing work invests further in this target.

Known prior: CEACAM5 (CEA) is a classical GI epithelial differentiation antigen — normal colon mucosal expression is expected and is not itself disqualifying (every anti-CEACAM5 ADC in clinical development already contends with this). The question this run is actually trying to answer is narrower: **does the bulk-RNA screen show meaningful expression outside the expected GI-mucosal compartments**, which would be a genuinely new liability signal, not a restatement of known CEA biology.
